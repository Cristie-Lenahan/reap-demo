About this demo

You have your own cart in this browser. Choose food, check the delivery price, then set up a test card on Reap’s secure page. Choosing food and checking your cart do not approve a payment.

Reloading keeps your cart. Closing a payment page does not cancel a test order. Trying the same order again returns the same receipt. After an order is complete, choose “Shop pet food again” to start a new cart and keep your old receipt.

What has been tested?

Product names, sizes, delivery prices and order results come from Reap’s sandbox, its test service. We request a simulated checkout. A completed test order does not prove a real payment, real stock or delivery, or that approval on the secure payment page has been fully tested.
